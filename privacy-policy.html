Privacy Policy & Data Deletion for Defence Guesser

Effective Date: 31 August 2026

Overview

Defence Guesser ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Defence Guesser application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of data when you use our App, and how you can request account or data deletion.

Information Collection and Use

By default, when playing as a guest, we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, IP address, location or device identifiers. The only data transmitted off your device as a guest is the self-chosen callsign and Daily Challenge score that you optionally submit to the global leaderboard.

If you choose to sign in with Google (optional), Google provides us with your name, email address, and profile picture URL to create your account profile and sync your game progress across devices.

Google Sign-In & Firebase Authentication

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose to sign in:

  • We store your name, email address, and unique Firebase User ID (UID) in Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.
  • Your email is used solely to identify your account and is never displayed publicly or shared with third parties.
  • Your game progress (scores, awards, history) is synced to your account.

Account & Data Deletion Request

If you signed in with Google or submitted leaderboard scores and wish to delete your account or any associated data:

  1. Send an email to: defenceguesser@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Account / Data Deletion Request
  3. Include your Google account email address or your leaderboard callsign.

We will permanently delete your Firebase account, profile data, and any synced game progress within 30 days of receiving your request.

You may also revoke Defence Guesser's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.

Game Data and Scores (Local Storage)

Game progress, high scores, Daily Challenge history, preferences, and your chosen callsign are stored locally on your device using standard browser Local Storage. You can clear this data at any time through your browser or device settings.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Leaflet – Interactive map library
  • CartoDB – Map tile provider
  • Google Fonts – Typography
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore & Authentication) – Cloud database and account authentication

Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy or data deletion, contact us at: defenceguesser@gmail.com.
